## Three benefit blocks

### Talk the way you think

You do not have to speak in tidy sentences. Start in the middle, change your mind, say "um". Eudemonia writes down what you said and cleans up the filler.

### Get notes you can use

Thoughts you capture close together are grouped into one organized note and filed under Work, Ideas, Todos or Journal. Choose a writing style, such as bullet points or professional. The details you spoke stay in the note.

### Keep it to yourself

Your recordings, transcripts and notes never leave your phone. There is no account to create and no server holding your words.

## How it works in 3 steps

1. **Record.** Tap the notification (it works from the lock screen), the home-screen widget, or the button in the app.
2. **Ramble.** Say whatever is on your mind. Tap stop. Your words are written down on your phone.
3. **Read.** Related thoughts are rewritten as one clean, organized note, ready when you are.

Subscriptions pay for servers. Eudemonia has no servers handling your notes, because the work happens on your phone. So there is no running cost to pass on to you. You pay once and keep what you bought.

### 2. Which phones does it work on?
