# Eudemonia — Demo Video Script

Draft v0.1, 2026-11-25 — not published; founder approval required before any use.

Script only — to be filmed when the app is ready.

Rules for both versions:
- Film on a real phone, with real app output. The clean note shown on screen must be what the app really produces from the spoken take. If the real output differs from the example below, change the script, never the footage.
- No volume buttons, no pocket shots, no screen-off capture, no "hands-free". Capture starts from the lock-screen notification, the widget, or the in-app button.
- No testimonials, numbers of users, or productivity claims. No exclamation marks in on-screen text.
- Voice: the founder, calm and plain. Music: soft and quiet, or none.
- Price and trial lines are placeholders until decided: [PRICE: $39.99, launch offer $29.99], [TRIAL: to be decided].

## The example thought (spoken once, off the cuff)

The speaker stands in a kitchen or on a walk, phone in hand, and says something like this, with real pauses and a change of mind. Do not read it from a script on screen; rehearse it until it sounds natural.

"Okay, so, um, for Thursday. I need to call Priya about the venue. No wait, first I need to check whether the budget is two thousand or twenty-five hundred, because that changes which venue. And, like, I keep thinking we should push the launch a week, so maybe tell Sam that before Friday."

The clean note this should become (illustrative; replace with the real output):

> **Work — Thursday planning**
> - Check the budget first: $2,000 or $2,500. It decides which venue.
> - Then call Priya about the venue.
> - Consider moving the launch back one week. Tell Sam before Friday.

Check on filming: every detail spoken (amounts, names, days, order of dependency) appears in the note unchanged.

## Notes for the founder and strategist

- Both versions end on the two lines the positioning calls for: price paid once, and privacy. Use "Nothing leaves your phone" only as an image of the real claim: "Your recordings and notes never leave your phone." Do not say "never connects to the internet".
- The app needs about 8 GB of memory for the organizing step. A one-line caption "Synthesis needs about 8 GB of memory" in the store-page version of the video (not required in the short vertical version) avoids a surprise. Decide where it goes.
- Show the trial or price only after the placeholders are final. Leave the price card out of the cut if they are not.
- Shoot the three starting methods (lock-screen notification, widget, in-app) as separate short inserts, so any one can be used in other clips later.
- Accessibility: add captions to every version. Many viewers watch without sound.
