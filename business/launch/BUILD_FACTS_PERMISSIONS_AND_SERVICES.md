# Eudemonia — Build Facts: Permissions, Background Service and Network Use (First Public Release)

Written by the engineering orchestrator on 2026-11-26 for the business workstream, at the founder's request, so that the Play Console texts in [`PLAY_SUBMISSION_TEXTS.md`](PLAY_SUBMISSION_TEXTS.md) can be checked against what the app really does. Engineering owns the facts in this file; the business workstream owns the wording submitted to Google. If a statement here and the build ever disagree, the build wins and this file must be corrected.

**State described**: the first-release build as of 2026-11-26 (capture from the lock screen and the shade verified on a Pixel 7a that day) (capture by notification, home-screen widget and in-app button; Capture and Synthesis only; model download inside the app; no Accessibility service; no battery-optimisation request). Items marked **[to verify on phone]** are built but not yet confirmed on a device.

## 1. Every Permission the Release Build Declares

| # | Permission | Kind | What the app uses it for | When the user meets it | Without it | Could we avoid it? | Google Play paperwork |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | `RECORD_AUDIO` (microphone) | Runtime prompt | Recording a spoken thought after the user taps Record (notification, widget or in-app button). Nothing else. No listening, no wake word, no recording on a schedule. | System prompt the first time the app is opened. | The app cannot do its one job. The Capture screen shows a banner with an `Allow` button. | **No.** Android gives no way to record sound without it. Handing off to the phone's own recorder app would lose the lock-screen capture, the transcript link and the single-tap flow. | Privacy policy must say why. Data safety form: audio is processed on the device and not collected. |
| 2 | `FOREGROUND_SERVICE` | Automatic (no prompt) | Android's general permission to run any foreground service; needed for row 3. | Never asked. | Row 3 cannot work. | No, it comes with row 3. | None on its own. |
| 3 | `FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE` + a service of type `microphone` | Automatic (no prompt), **declared and reviewed by Google** | Lets recording continue when Eudemonia is not on screen: recording started from the lock screen, from the notification shade inside another app, from the home-screen widget, or carrying on after the screen turns off. Android blocks the microphone for any app that is not on screen unless it runs a foreground service of exactly this type with a visible notification. | The user sees its notification: `Record Thought` when idle, a running timer and `Stop & Save` while recording. | Recording would only work while the app's own screen is open and lit. No lock-screen capture, no widget capture, and a recording would be cut when the screen turns off. | 4 | `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` | Runtime prompt (Android 13+) | Showing the persistent notification that carries the Record button (lock screen and shade) and the recording timer. The notification is in a normal-importance category that never makes a sound or vibrates; normal importance is needed because many phones hide "silent" notifications on the lock screen (confirmed on the test phone). | System prompt the first time the app is opened. | The service still runs, but its notification is hidden, so there is no lock-screen Record button; the widget and the in-app button still work. The Capture screen shows a banner with `Turn on`. | No, if the lock-screen button is wanted. The widget alone would work without it. | None beyond normal use. No marketing or reminder notifications are sent. |
| 4b | `RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED` | Automatic | Putting the Record notification back after the phone restarts (and, through a related system signal, after an app update). The app only re-posts the notification; **it does not start its service, open the microphone or do any work at boot**. The service starts when the user next taps Record. | Never asked. | After a restart of the phone the lock-screen Record button would be missing until the app is opened once. | Yes, at that cost. | 5 | `WAKE_LOCK` | Automatic | Keeps the processor awake, for a bounded time, while a transcription or a Synthesis run is in progress with the screen off, so the work finishes instead of stalling half-way. | 6 | `VIBRATE` | Automatic | A short buzz when a recording starts and a double buzz when it is saved, so the user knows without looking. | Never asked. | No haptic confirmation. | Yes, but it would hurt eyes-free use. | None. |
| 7 | `INTERNET` | Automatic | **Only** downloading the on-device model files: the speech model (about 640 MB) and the language model the user chooses (about 1 to 2.5 GB), from Hugging Face over HTTPS, each checked against a known fingerprint before use. No recording, transcript, note, identifier or analytics is ever sent. There is no account and no server of ours. | The user starts each download by tapping; Android shows its own download notification. | A phone that installs from Play would have no models: it could record but not transcribe or write notes. | Only by shipping the models inside the install (the speech model could later go into a Play asset pack; the language model is too large for one pack). | Privacy policy and Data safety form must describe the download (the host sees the phone's network address, like any download). **This replaces the earlier statement that the app has no network permission at all.** |

**Also present, added automatically by Android libraries**: `com.eudemonia.eudemonia.DYNAMIC_RECEIVER_NOT_EXPORTED_PERMISSION` (an internal marker that protects the app's own internal messages; it grants nothing) and a `queries` entry for text selection (`PROCESS_TEXT`, lets the standard copy/share menu appear on selected text).

**Added 2026-11-27 without any new permission**: a Quick Settings tile (`Record thought`) that starts and stops a recording the same way the notification and the widget do (it needs the system's `BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE` protection on the app's own tile service, which is not a permission the app requests); and a request for audio focus while recording, which asks other apps to pause their sound so music is not recorded with the voice. Neither appears in the permission list and neither needs a Play declaration.

## 2. Removed or Never Requested

| Capability | Status | Why |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| Accessibility service (volume-button trigger) | **Removed from this release**; code parked for later. The built app's manifest contains no Accessibility declaration (checked). | Long Play review and needs testing across many phones. |
| `REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS` (battery-optimisation exemption) | **Removed** (founder decision 2026-11-26). | Play restricts it and lists no voice-recording use. With the foreground service plus the bounded wake lock, processing continues with the screen off on standard Android **[to verify on phone, unplugged]**. Known cost: some manufacturers' aggressive battery savers may stop background work sooner; the recording itself is not at risk, only how soon it is transcribed. |
| Starting the app's screen from the background | **Never done.** No permission exists or is needed. A tap on Record never opens the app or puts it in front of what the user is doing, and the app is never shown over the lock screen. | Product requirement; also what Android 12+ enforces. |
| Storage / files / media permissions | Not requested. | Everything is kept in the app's own private folders. |
| Location, contacts, calendar, camera, phone, SMS, Bluetooth, overlay ("draw over other apps"), exact alarms | Not requested. | Not used. |
| Advertising ID, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs | None in the build. | Nothing leaves the phone except the model download request. |

## 3. The One Real Choice: How Long the Microphone Service Runs

The microphone permission and a microphone-type foreground service are unavoidable for capture from the lock screen, the shade and the widget (rows 1 and 3). What is a choice is the service's **lifetime**.

| | A. Always on (what the build does) | B. Only while recording, plus a second service while processing |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| What runs | One service of type `microphone` from the moment the app is first opened (and the microphone is granted) until the user switches it off in Settings. It shows the persistent notification. The microphone itself is opened only between a Record tap and a Stop tap. | A plain notification with the Record button. A `microphone` service only between Record and Stop. A second service of type `dataSync` (Android's category for local file processing) only while transcribing or writing notes. |
| Capture | Instant: the app's process is already alive. | First tap after a long idle starts the app's process first; a short delay at the start of a recording is possible. |
| Transcription and Synthesis with the app closed | Work in the background, including the automatic run after a quiet period, so notes are ready when the user opens the app. | Work only when started while the app is open or right after a recording; the automatic run after a quiet period would wait until the app is next opened. |
| Fit with Google's wording ("started by the user, noticeable, can be stopped") | Weaker: the service is running while nothing is being recorded. A reviewer may ask why. Honest answer: it hosts the Record button and keeps on-device processing alive; the microphone is used only after a tap. | Stronger: each service runs exactly while its task runs. Needs a **second declaration** (`dataSync`) and Android 15 limits that type to 6 hours a day. |
| Engineering cost and risk | None, it is built and has run for weeks on the test phone. | Several days of work in Android's most restrictive area, right before launch. |

**Engineering recommendation**: ship A, describe it truthfully in the declaration (one service hosts the persistent notification and the recording; the microphone is opened only after the user taps Record), and keep B as the prepared answer if Google objects. B does not reduce what the user is asked to grant; it only changes how long the service is alive, at a real cost to "notes are ready when I open the app".

**For the declaration text** (business workstream to word): the bracketed founder question in `PLAY_SUBMISSION_TEXTS.md` section (a) is answered as: *one* service, type `microphone`, is used for both the persistent notification and the recording; there is no second service type in this build. Transcription and note writing happen in the same app process while that service is running. The video should be recorded on Android 14 or later.

## 4. Other Facts the Submission Texts Depend On

- **Model download**: started by the user; uses Android's system download service, which shows its own notification and resumes after a lost connection; Wi-Fi only by default with a visible choice to allow mobile data **[to verify on phone]**. Files come from fixed versions on Hugging Face and are rejected unless their SHA-256 fingerprint matches. Third parties host these files; a mirror under our own account would remove the risk of a file being withdrawn.
- **Device check**: the app reads the phone's total memory and only lets the user pick language models that fit; on a phone where none fits, Capture and transcription still work and Synthesis shows an honest message. This is a statement the listing may use only after it is verified on a low-memory phone.
- **Model licences** (the app should list them in Settings before release): speech model NVIDIA Parakeet TDT 0.6b v3 (CC-BY-4.0, attribution required); Qwen3 language models (Apache-2.0); inference libraries llama.cpp (MIT) and sherpa-onnx (Apache-2.0).
- **Android backup**: undecided. Android's automatic cloud backup is on by default and would copy the notes database to the user's Google account; that conflicts with "nothing leaves the phone" unless it is switched off in the build or disclosed. Off means notes do not move to a new phone automatically.
- **Recordings made while the app is fully closed** are saved at once and transcribed the next time the app is opened.
