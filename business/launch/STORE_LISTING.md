Ramble. Get clean, organized notes. Nothing leaves your phone.

Eudemonia turns spoken thoughts into tidy written notes. Say what is on your mind in whatever order it comes. The app writes down your words, then turns the mess into a clear note, filed in the right folder. Everything happens on your phone. There is no account and no cloud processing.

HOW IT WORKS

1. Capture. Start a recording from the persistent notification (it works from the lock screen), from a home-screen widget, or inside the app. Say your thought. Tap stop. Nothing stands between you and the record button.

2. Transcribe. Your speech is turned into text on your phone. Filler words and false starts are cleaned up. You can play back the original audio at any time, and edit the text if you want to.

3. Synthesize. Thoughts you capture close together are grouped and rewritten as one organized note. Each note is filed in one folder: Work, Ideas, Todos or Journal. Pick a writing style, such as bullet points, concise or professional. Merge notes, or move a single thought to a different note.

WHAT IS FREE AND WHAT IS PAID

Free forever: recording, transcription, audio playback and editing, on any supported phone.

Synthesis, the step that turns rambling speech into clean, organized notes, is free during a trial: [TRIAL: to be decided — e.g. first N syntheses or N days]. After that it is a single purchase of [PRICE: $39.99, launch offer $29.99]. There is no subscription, ever, for what you buy. Pay once and keep it.

Why a one-time price? Many voice note apps charge $40 to $100 every year, often because every minute you speak costs them money on a server. Eudemonia has no servers, because the work happens on your phone.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Speech-to-text and the language model both run on your phone. Your recordings, transcripts and notes never leave your phone. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to. Purchases go through Google Play.

WHAT YOU NEED

Recording and transcription: [MINIMUM ANDROID VERSION AND MEMORY: to be confirmed].

Synthesis needs a phone with about 8 GB of memory. It also needs a one-time download of about 2.5 GB, so use Wi-Fi and keep enough free storage. After the download, Synthesis works without a connection. If your phone has less memory, you can still record, transcribe, play back and edit for free.

Eudemonia works in English only for now.

CAPTURE FROM WHERE YOU ARE

The persistent notification has a Record button that works from the lock screen. The home-screen widget starts a recording with one tap. Inside the app, a large button sits at the bottom of the screen. Recording never waits for anything to load.

ONE PERSON, ONE APP

Eudemonia is made by one person. If something does not work on your phone, write to [SUPPORT EMAIL] and you will get a reply from the person who built it.
