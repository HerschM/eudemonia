## Wispr Flow (Wispr AI)

**1. What / platforms.** Cloud AI dictation: speak in any app, cleaned text is inserted. Mac, Windows, iPhone (keyboard), Android. Dictation: Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android; Notetaker (meetings): Mac, Windows, iPhone, Android "on the roadmap" [OFFICIAL, https://wisprflow.ai/pricing, 2026-11-24]. Timeline: Mac Oct 2024, Windows Mar 2025, iOS June 2025 [ESTIMATE, https://www.postbeam.ai/blog/how-wisprflow-grows]; iOS June 3, 2025 [PRESS, TechCrunch via https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wispr_Flow]. **Android launched on Google Play 2026-02-23**, floating bubble that works alongside the existing keyboard [PRESS, https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/23/wispr-flow-launches-an-android-app-for-ai-powered-dictation]. Requires Android 13+ and an internet connection ("Flow does not work offline") [OFFICIAL, https://wisprflow.ai/android, undated]. Android waitlist drew 375,000 verified signups in under a week [PRESS, summarised by search result from https://thebridge.jp/2026/09/wispr-flow-series-b-280m-funding; single secondary source, not independently confirmed].

**2. Processing / privacy.** Cloud. "Flow requires an internet connection to transcribe your speech" [OFFICIAL, https://wisprflow.ai/android]. Privacy Mode = zero data retention (audio processed then discarded); model-training is a separate toggle; cloud dictation history storage is a separate setting [OFFICIAL, https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/6274675613-privacy-mode-data-retention, dated ~March 2026]. A third-party guide says Privacy Mode is off by default for non-HIPAA users [ESTIMATE/competitor blog, https://www.getvoibe.com/resources/is-wispr-flow-safe/]. 25-30% of users opt in to let data train models [FOUNDER, https://www.computerworld.com/article/4107331/wispr-ceo-interview-post-keyboard-office.html, 2025-12-31]. Compliance: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, HIPAA [PRESS, https://thenextweb.com/news/wispr-series-b-280m-2bn-valuation-menlo-canto, Aug 2026].

**3. Pricing (fetched 2026-11-24, https://wisprflow.ai/pricing) [OFFICIAL].**
- Free $0: Dictation and Notetaker "with limited weekly usage"; no card needed. Meeting history 30 days on desktop. Recordings under 5 min do not count toward the meeting limit.
- Pro: $15/user/mo monthly, $12/user/mo billed annually (20% off).
- Growth (team): page text shows $23 and $18 (fetch summariser said it was unclear; $33/$26 also appear, likely struck-through list prices). Treat Growth exact price as AMBIGUOUS.
- Enterprise: custom, annual only.
- Students/nonprofits: page says Flow is free for students and nonprofits [as summarised]; Pro for students 50% off, educators 40% off. Conflicts with the help centre, see section 7.
- Lifetime: NOT FOUND on page. A competitor blog states there is no lifetime deal ("Not Yet") [ESTIMATE, https://www.getvoibe.com/resources/wispr-flow-lifetime-deal/].
- Gated by payment: unlimited dictation words, unlimited Notetaker, shared dictionary/snippets, team workspace, admin/billing, analytics, priority support, early access. Growth adds SSO/SAML, org-wide HIPAA + BAA, model-training admin control. Enterprise adds SCIM, audit logs, MDM, SLAs.
- Free limit exact numbers: pricing page stopped listing them around 2026-11-01 ("Limited words per week") [ESTIMATE, https://pricingsaas.com/news/article/wispr-flow-drops-numeric-free-plan-limits-makes-pro-meetings-unlimited]. Last known numbers: desktop 2,000 words/week [PRESS, https://thenextweb.com/news/wispr-series-b-280m-2bn-valuation-menlo-canto, Aug 2026]; Android cut to 1,000/week on 2026-09-21, then numbers made vague 2026-10-20 [ESTIMATE, pricingsaas link above]. Official help article now gives no numbers; limit applies only to Free accounts without an active Google Play subscription; resets Sundays; active trials have no limit [OFFICIAL, https://docs.wisprflow.ai/articles/4760791189-free-tier-weekly-word-cap-and-bonus-words-remove-desktop-trial-experiment, undated "updated 2 days ago"]. At Android launch, "unlimited dictation on Android free for all users" was stated [per search summary of https://wisprflow.ai/android; later superseded by the cap above].
- Android billing: Google Play subscriptions exist (per help article above). Play price level NOT FOUND (Play page not retrievable).

**4. Funding / team.** $4.5M seed (NEA, 8VC) 2021 [ESTIMATE, postbeam]; $30M Series A June 2025 led by Menlo, total then $56M [OFFICIAL, https://wisprflow.ai/post/wispr-raises-series-a, 2025-06-24]; $25M extension Nov 2025 led by Notable Capital at $700M post-money, total $81M [PRESS, https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/23/wispr-flow-launches-an-android-app-for-ai-powered-dictation]; **$280M Series B at $2B valuation, led by Menlo, announced 2026-08-17; total $361M**; investors: Notable, NEA, Neo, 8VC, MVP (existing), Acrew, Forerunner, Goodwater, Peak XV, Together Fund, PLUS Capital (new) [PRESS, https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/17/wispr-raises-280m-at-2b-valuation-as-it-looks-beyond-dictation/]. Forge Global lists a different total ($334M) [ESTIMATE, via search result]. Team: 7 to 60 employees in year to May 2026 [FOUNDER claim as relayed by https://www.postbeam.ai/blog/how-wisprflow-grows; unverified]; headcount in Aug 2026 NOT FOUND (TechCrunch/TNW: not disclosed). Founded 2021 by Tanay Kothari and Sahaj Garg; originally a wearable, pivoted to Flow software [PRESS, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wispr_Flow].

**5. Traction.**
- Revenue growth ">150% for four consecutive quarters" [PRESS, https://fortune.com/2026/08/17/wispr-2-billion-valuation-dictations-only-the-beginning/]; "millions" of consumer users, 100,000 businesses (Fortune) vs "tens of thousands" (Menlo) vs ">10,000 enterprises" (Reuters): sources do not reconcile [PRESS, https://thenextweb.com/news/wispr-series-b-280m-2bn-valuation-menlo-canto].
- ARR NOT officially disclosed. Estimates: ~$10M ARR Oct 2025 [ESTIMATE, https://getlatka.com/companies/wisprflow.ai and postbeam]; ~US$3.8M revenue July 2024-July 2025 [ESTIMATE, Wikipedia, unconfirmed]. These two conflict (different periods; ARR run-rate vs trailing revenue).
- Conversion/retention: ~19% payment rate, >50% monthly user growth, ~80% six-month retention [ESTIMATE, Wikipedia citing company-reported data, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wispr_Flow]. 80% six-month retention for activated users [OFFICIAL, https://wisprflow.ai/post/wispr-raises-series-a, June 2025]. The 19% figure has no named primary source.
- Founder claim: 150x revenue, 200x user growth in year to May 2026 [FOUNDER via postbeam, unverified].
- Geography: ~40% US, ~30% Europe; India #2 market by Apr 2026 but 14% of downloads vs 2% of revenue (Oct 2025-Apr 2026) [ESTIMATE, postbeam; company data, source unspecified].
- Traffic ~4.6-5.5M monthly visits spring 2026 [ESTIMATE, Similarweb via postbeam].

**6. Acquisition channels.** Word of mouth/product-led (revenue and users ~50% MoM in 2025, "driven mainly by word of mouth") and ~125 new enterprise customers/week, no outbound sales [FOUNDER, https://www.computerworld.com/article/4107331/wispr-ceo-interview-post-keyboard-office.html, 2025-12-31]. Per a third-party analysis [ESTIMATE, https://www.postbeam.ai/blog/how-wisprflow-grows]: celebrity/investor endorsements (Hoffman, Andreessen, Wozniak), referral (give a month Pro, get a month), founder content on X/LinkedIn, UGC campaign (claims 500M views in 60 days, FOUNDER claim), paid ads on Meta/Google/LinkedIn (ad-library observation), SEO mostly brand search, India localisation (Hinglish model, Rs 320/mo pricing, ~30 local staff). Wispr scaled go-to-market in India and UK [PRESS, TechCrunch Aug 2026].

**7. Pricing changes / controversies.**
- Free-limit erosion: mobile cap 1,000 words/week on iPhone (older), Android cut to 1,000 on 2026-09-21, numeric caps hidden by 2026-11-01 [ESTIMATE, https://pricingsaas.com/news/article/wispr-flow-drops-numeric-free-plan-limits-makes-pro-meetings-unlimited]. Help centre figures conflict between sources (some say 550/week iPhone).
- Student plan: ~2026-07-30 a free unlimited-words student plan appeared, withdrawn ~2026-08-05, reverted to 3 months free then $6/mo (50% off) [ESTIMATE, https://www.usagepricing.com/blueprint/activity/wisprflow-2026-08-11-student-plan-reverted]. The current pricing page again says free for students; conflicts with that tracker (page may have changed since).
- Privacy: 2025 reports (secondary, competitor-authored) that the app sent active-window screenshots; Wispr confirmed the app can read keystrokes (for context) [PRESS-ish, https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wispr_Flow]. No regulator finding found. Treat screenshot story as UNVERIFIED primary.
- Quality dip reported by users around Aug 2026; new proprietary "Canto" model announced [PRESS, TechCrunch Aug 2026]. TechCrunch notes competitors offering free/cheaper prosumer tools = pricing pressure.
- Google launched a free offline dictation app on iOS (Eloquent, see Android section); TNW headline frames it as making $15/mo hard to justify [PRESS, https://thenextweb.com/news/google-offline-dictation-app-ios, April 2026].

**8. Implication.** The category leader is cloud-only, monetises with $12-15/mo plus enterprise seats, and is already tightening its free tier on Android; an offline Android app can credibly sell "works with no internet, no audio leaves the phone", which Wispr structurally cannot match. But Wispr's money comes from team/enterprise features (SSO, HIPAA, admin), which a solo on-device app will not have; the individual-consumer price anchor is $12-15/mo.

## Superwhisper

**1. What / platforms.** Dictation + meeting transcription app; Mac, Windows, iOS, Android [OFFICIAL, https://superwhisper.com, fetched 2026-11-24; Meetings Mac only]. **Android app exists on Google Play** ("Superwhisper - AI Dictation", SuperUltra, Inc., package com.superultra.superwhisper): 5K+ downloads, 4.0 stars from 102 reviews, in-app purchases [OFFICIAL, https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.superultra.superwhisper, as read 2026-11-24 via summariser]. Listing "updated" Oct 28 / Nov 5, 2026 in two cached versions; floating bubble alongside existing keyboard [search result of the Play listing]. Android launch date NOT FOUND (no press release/changelog entry; as late as April 2026 a third-party said no Android app, and the feedback board still had it as top-voted pending request) [ESTIMATE, https://www.getvoibe.com/resources/superwhisper-platform-support/; https://feedback.superwhisper.com/board/p/android-app]. So the Android app most likely launched mid/late 2026.

**2. Processing / privacy.** Hybrid, user-selectable. Free tier uses local Whisper models: "Nothing you dictate leaves your device"; Pro unlocks cloud voice models and BYO API keys [OFFICIAL, https://superwhisper.com/docs/billing/plans]. Offline models run well only on Apple Silicon Macs [OFFICIAL, https://superwhisper.com]. Enterprise is SOC 2 Type II. Android specifics: a Play reviewer wished for a "fully offline privacy focused transcription option" [search snippet of Play reviews, so Android may be cloud-dependent; UNVERIFIED].

**3. Pricing** [OFFICIAL, https://superwhisper.com/docs/billing/plans, fetched 2026-11-24; undated doc].
- Free: unlimited dictation with local Whisper models, custom vocab, up to 2 modes without AI processing.
- Pro: **$8.49/mo, $84.99/yr (about 17% off), $249.99 lifetime**. "Every plan includes the same features; only the billing differs."
- New-user trial: 3,000 words of Pro.
- Pro-only: AI modes, cloud models, Parakeet/Cohere local models, context awareness, file transcription, realtime, system audio, speaker separation, Meetings (macOS), BYO keys, priority support.
- Students/educators/non-profits: 40% off via email proof; not valid for App Store purchases.
- Enterprise: seat licensing, SSO, central deployment; price not listed.
- One Pro licence covers unlimited Macs and PCs plus iPhone and Android. Android/iOS purchases via store are priced higher due to store fees; website is cheapest.
- Conflict: competitor blogs claim lifetime jumped to $849; the official docs show $249.99. The $849 likely comes from the home page text "$849" which is $8.49 with the decimal lost in rendering. Treat claim as FALSE/unreliable. Earlier lifetime was $199 [ESTIMATE, via search summary of competitor blog].

**4. Funding / team.** Bootstrapped, no outside investment as of May 2026 [ESTIMATE, https://aiwiki.ai/wiki/superwhisper; unverified wiki]. Founder Neil Chudleigh (PartnerStack co-founder), Toronto; six full-time staff plus five contractors [ESTIMATE, aiwiki]; first employee hired Aug 2025 [ESTIMATE, aiwiki]. Brendan James head of growth [PRESS, https://betakit.com/superwhisper-cohere-partner-on-meeting-recorder-to-challenge-enterprise-clients/, 2026-10-15].

**5. Traction.** Site claim "hundreds of thousands" of people use it [OFFICIAL, https://superwhisper.com]. Chudleigh reportedly told The Globe and Mail "hundreds of thousands of weekly active users" and "seven-figure" revenue [ESTIMATE, aiwiki relaying Globe and Mail; original not retrieved]. Revenue mix: previously 80-90% individual, ~40% enterprise now [ESTIMATE, aiwiki citing BetaKit; BetaKit article itself says "mostly individual, increasingly enterprise" without percentages, https://betakit.com/...]. Conversion/retention NOT FOUND. Android: 5K+ installs.

**6. Acquisition.** Founder-led social, high-profile testimonials (Karpathy, Pieter Levels, Andrew Wilkinson, Guillermo Rauch) [OFFICIAL, superwhisper.com]; developers dictating into AI coding tools [PRESS, BetaKit]; Cohere partnership (Oct 2026) for Canadian enterprise. Product Hunt presence (iOS launch listing; details NOT FOUND).

**7. Changes/controversies.** Lifetime price raised from $199 to $249 at some point [ESTIMATE, competitor blog]. Shift toward enterprise revenue [PRESS, BetaKit]. No privacy incident found.

**8. Implication.** Closest existing model to what a solo developer can run: free local tier is unlimited (acts as acquisition), money from Pro features and a lifetime option at about 30x the monthly price. Their Android app is thin (5K installs) so the Android on-device segment is open.

## Willow Voice

**1. What / platforms.** Cloud-first AI dictation, Mac, Windows, iPhone; **Android is a waitlist ("coming soon"), not released** [OFFICIAL, https://willowvoice.com and https://willowvoice.com/pricing, 2026-11-24]. TechCrunch (2025-11-12) said Windows and Android expansion planned "in coming months" [PRESS, https://techcrunch.com/2025/11/12/willows-voice-keyboard-lets-you-type-across-all-your-ios-apps-and-actually-edit-what-you-said]: Android has slipped >12 months.

**2. Processing / privacy.** Site claims SOC 2 Type II, HIPAA, zero data retention, end-to-end encryption, and an "offline mode" that runs on-device [OFFICIAL, https://willowvoice.com]. Competitor blogs say it is cloud-processed; TNW (April 2026) says Willow "relies on cloud processing" [PRESS, https://thenextweb.com/news/google-offline-dictation-app-ios].

**3. Pricing** [OFFICIAL, https://willowvoice.com/pricing, 2026-11-24].
- Free (Basic): $0; unlimited lighter model ("Frontier Mini"), limited personalisation, 20 Scribe uses/week. (An older competitor blog says 2,000 words/week; conflict, official page used.)
- Pro: $15/mo monthly, $12/mo billed annually ("Save 20%"). Adds stronger model, priority transcription, style memory, unlimited Scribe, longer dictation.
- Business (Teams): $35/mo monthly, $28/mo annual (page marks it per user via "Get teams"; unit ambiguity in the fetch). Adds enforced zero-data-retention, SOC 2, enforced HIPAA, admin controls.
- Enterprise: custom (invoice/PO, SSO/SAML, MSA/DPA, API, SLAs).
- Student: discounts for students, educators, nonprofits, veterans on Individual Pro, no percentage on page (a competitor blog says 30%).
- Lifetime: not offered.
- Note: the fetch summariser's reading of the pricing page's column alignment was flagged as unclear for tier-to-feature mapping.

**4. Funding / team.** $4.5M total seed from Box Group, Y Combinator, Burst Capital, angels including Dharmesh Shah and Alexis Ohanian [PRESS, TechCrunch 2025-11-12]. Another outlet reports "$4.2M seed" led by BoxGroup with Goodwater, Burst, Liquid 2 [PRESS, https://dealroom.co/news/146403-willow-voice-raises-4-2m-seed-to-turn-voice-into-a-computer-interface/; the page date reads Aug 23, 2026, which looks inconsistent with TechCrunch's Nov 2025 date; unreconciled]. YC S24, original co-founders left; CEO Allan Guo (age 20), co-founder Lawrence Liu. Headcount NOT FOUND.

**5. Traction.** "150,000+ professionals", 600+ US App Store reviews [OFFICIAL, https://willowvoice.com, self-reported]. "Grown 50% month-over-month in users" [PRESS, TechCrunch 2025-11-12]. ARR ~$550K late 2025/early 2026 [ESTIMATE, aggregator via search, low confidence]. Enterprise customers named: Uber, Heidi Health, Zego [PRESS, TechCrunch].

**6. Acquisition.** YC network, iOS keyboard launch coverage, enterprise teams with shared vocab. Detailed channels NOT FOUND.

**7. Changes/controversies.** No major incident found. Android promised and delayed.

**8. Implication.** Venture-funded and small ($4.5M, tiny ARR estimate) vs Wispr's scale; shows how hard cloud dictation is to differentiate. Same $12/$15 price points as Wispr; Android demand is explicit but they have not shipped.

## Aqua Voice

**3. Pricing** [OFFICIAL, https://aquavoice.com/pricing, 2026-11-24].
- Free: 2,000 words (the page says "2,000 words"; per-week vs one-time not stated), first 3 Notetaker meetings, Avalon 1.5, Deep Context, MCP/CLI. Android release says "first 1,000 words free" [OFFICIAL, Business Wire]: conflict.

- Max: $24/mo, $19/mo annual. Unlimited meetings, Realtime Mode, voice commands, early access, BAA.
- Business: $24/seat/mo, $19/seat/mo annual ($228/yr), min 2 seats; SSO, SCIM, ZDR, BAA. >20 users contact sales.
- Student: 70% off Pro and Max (Pro $3/mo annual).

**4. Funding / team.** YC W24; founders Finnian Brown (CEO) and Jack McIntire (CTO) [OFFICIAL, Business Wire]. Funding amounts conflict and are low-quality: $500K pre-seed (VCBacked) vs $3.25M total (Caplight) [ESTIMATE, https://www.caplight.com/company/withaqua]. No amount/valuation disclosed by the company. **6. Acquisition.** Technical credibility (leaderboard ranking on Open ASR, 9to5Mac comparison), YC. A "Aqua Pet"/leaderboard gamification exists for paid plans [OFFICIAL, pricing page]. Other channels NOT FOUND.

**7. Changes.** Free tier is now a words cap rather than a time-limited trial; hardware "Aqua Pen" bundled. No controversy found.

**8. Implication.** Mid-priced ($10-12/mo) with a very low free cap (1,000-2,000 words) as a paywall nudge; student discount at 70% is aggressive. Shows even small players ship Android as a floating bubble rather than a keyboard.

## Typeless

**1. What / platforms.** AI voice keyboard/dictation: macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, Android [OFFICIAL, https://www.typeless.com/pricing and https://www.typeless.com, 2026-11-24]. **Android launched January 2026** (Product Hunt Launch of the Day on 2026-01-20) [ESTIMATE/PRESS, https://www.producthunt.com/p/undefined/we-are-launching-typeless-for-andorid-ask-me-anything via search result; TechCrunch confirms "launched an app for the platform last month" in Feb 2026, https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/23/wispr-flow-launches-an-android-app-for-ai-powered-dictation].

**2. Processing / privacy.** **Cloud**, despite "on-device" marketing wording. Official data-controls page: "Transcription is performed on the cloud to ensure the highest accuracy and low-latency performance"; audio not stored; history text stays on device unless cloud sync enabled; processes app context and nearby text [OFFICIAL, https://www.typeless.com/data-controls, fetched 2026-11-24]. The pricing page says "processing is described as on-device" in the summariser's reading (the pricing page ambiguity), and the maker on Product Hunt said "all with on-device privacy" [maker comment, Product Hunt]. Interpretation by competitor blog: "on-device" applies only to history storage [ESTIMATE, competitor-authored, https://www.getvoibe.com/resources/typeless-privacy-issues/]. Unverified allegations (plaintext local DB of URLs, broad permissions) from a Japanese engineer analysis, Nov 2025, relayed secondhand; company name not disclosed in terms [same source]. HIPAA compliance announced March 2026; no public BAA found by Paubox [same competitor blog].

**3. Pricing** [OFFICIAL, https://www.typeless.com/pricing, 2026-11-24].
- Free: $0, limited words weekly (number not on page), standard accuracy, standard access during high demand.
- Pro: **$12/mo billed yearly, $30/mo billed monthly**. Unlimited words, enhanced accuracy, priority access, cloud sync, team tools.
- Ultra: $200/mo ("Overkill, by design"): max accuracy.
- Enterprise: custom (SSO, SCIM, audit logs, HIPAA).
- Student: a "Typeless for students" page exists; terms NOT FOUND.
- Lifetime: none.
- Note the unusually big gap between monthly ($30) and annual ($12): strong push to annual.

**4. Funding / team.** NOT FOUND. Company site: "backed by a startup accelerator focused on Stanford students, professors, alumni" (unnamed); Huang Song is Founder & CEO; operated by Simply CA LLC, Palo Alto [OFFICIAL, https://www.typeless.com/about]. An aggregator says StartX [ESTIMATE, https://www.everydev.ai/developers/typeless].

**5. Traction.** No user counts or revenue found. Claims "#1 Product of the Day" on Product Hunt for desktop and mobile [OFFICIAL, typeless.com].

**6. Acquisition.** Product Hunt launches (desktop and Android), social accounts (X, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram), "Typeless for students" page [OFFICIAL, typeless.com]. Channel effectiveness NOT FOUND.

**7. Controversies.** Privacy-claim ambiguity as above (Nov 2025 reverse-engineering thread; secondhand).

**8. Implication.** Typeless shows the reputational risk of using "on-device" wording while sending audio to the cloud; an app that truly has zero network permission can make a verifiable claim (no INTERNET permission) that rivals cannot. Note $200 "Ultra" tier as a price-anchoring decoy.

## VoiceInk

**1. What / platforms.** Open-source (GPL-3.0) on-device dictation for macOS 15+; site also promotes an iOS version (no details) [OFFICIAL, https://tryvoiceink.com and https://github.com/Beingpax/VoiceInk, 2026-11-24]. **No Android** (NOT FOUND).

Free trial (length not stated). No subscription, no team or student pricing listed. Building from source is free but excludes updates and priority support [OFFICIAL, GitHub].

**7. Changes.** None found.

**8. Implication.** Strong evidence that an open-source/local app with a $25-49 one-time licence has real paying demand; "open source but paid convenience (updates, support)" is a viable licensing pattern. Mac-only.

### A. Google AI Edge Eloquent (Google) - not yet on Android
- iOS App Store April 6, 2026; free, "voice dictation without subscriptions", no usage caps; offline Gemma-based ASR on device; optional cloud mode uses Gemini for text cleanup [PRESS, https://thenextweb.com/news/google-offline-dictation-app-ios; 9to5Google https://9to5google.com/2026/04/06/google-ai-edge-eloquent-app/ via search result]. App Store listing references Android; no Android release found as of search (Android Authority review said iOS and macOS only) [PRESS, search summaries; status for Nov 2026 NOT FOUND].
- Business model: none directly (free, Google platform strategy). Threat: free + offline + big brand on the exact on-device Android niche if Android release arrives.

### B. FUTO Voice Input / FUTO Keyboard (Android-only, on-device)
- Android only; Google Play, F-Droid, direct APK (~70 MB), v2.1 [OFFICIAL, https://voiceinput.futo.tech/]. Claims "designed to work entirely on-device with no data stored".
- Business model: one-time license purchase ("also valid for FUTO Keyboard"), plus nag to "Help Us Keep Running!"; price on page NOT FOUND; AlternativeTo says $5 and also "free", internally inconsistent [ESTIMATE, https://alternativeto.net/software/futo-voice-input/about]. Source-first licence (non-commercial) [ESTIMATE, https://scancode-licensedb.aboutcode.org/futo-sfl-1.0.html]. Funded by FUTO (organisation) rather than a startup; traction NOT FOUND.
- Implication: an Android on-device dictation precedent where the app is basically free-to-use, voluntary or tiny payment. Sets a low price ceiling on pure "dictation".

### C. Dictus (open source, Android)
- On-device Android keyboard with Whisper/Parakeet, MIT licence, free dictation; "Dictus Pro" (on-device LLM "Smart Modes") in development, price not set; donations do not unlock Pro; sideload APK from GitHub (32 stars) [OFFICIAL, https://github.com/getdictus/dictus-android, fetched 2026-11-24]. Its comparison table lists Wispr "Free / $15/mo", Superwhisper "Free / $8.49/mo".
- Implication: tiny but shows the planned model for on-device Android: free dictation, paid on-device LLM rewrite. This is exactly the neighbourhood the client's local-LLM structuring would sit in.

### Patterns

1. **Price anchor for cloud dictation is $12-15/mo (annual $10-12/mo).** Wispr, Willow ($15/$12), Aqua ($12/$10), Typeless ($30 monthly / $12 annual) cluster on exactly the same numbers; Aqua and Typeless run higher "Max/Ultra" tiers ($24, $200) as anchors. Local-first indie tools charge less ($8.49 monthly Superwhisper) or one-time ($25-49 VoiceInk, EUR 64 MacWhisper, $249.99 Superwhisper lifetime). Only Superwhisper offers monthly, annual, and lifetime together.
2. **Free tier = word cap that tightens over time.** Wispr cut Android's free cap to 1,000/week in Sept 2026 and then stopped publishing numbers; Aqua's is 1,000-2,000 words. Local-first apps instead give unlimited free use of a basic local model and gate AI features, cloud models, and meetings. Their gate is quality/features, not volume, since volume costs them nothing.
3. **Where revenue actually comes from: teams.** Wispr, Willow, Aqua, Typeless all reserve SSO/SCIM, HIPAA/BAA, zero-retention enforcement, audit logs, admin billing for higher tiers. Superwhisper's mix shifted toward enterprise (~40% by one second-hand report). A single-user on-device app will not have these levers.
4. **Cloud is dominant; "on-device" is a marketing contested term.** Typeless's own data-controls page says transcription is in the cloud while the pricing page/maker comments say "on-device"; this produced privacy backlash. Wispr needs internet and has had screenshot/keystroke-context scrutiny. Only Superwhisper (free local), MacWhisper, VoiceInk, FUTO, Dictus, Eloquent (offline mode) are genuinely local; on Android the genuinely local options are free or near-free, tiny, and mostly open-source (FUTO, Dictus, Eloquent not yet).
5. **Android is new for everyone in 2026.** Typeless Jan 2026, Wispr Feb 2026, Aqua Oct 2026, Superwhisper roughly Q3-Q4 2026 (undated), Willow still waitlist, Google Eloquent iOS-first. All use the floating-bubble approach (Wispr, Superwhisper, Aqua) or a keyboard. None market a "press hardware buttons with screen off" trigger or structured notes/tasks output; all are "type into the active text field" products. This is the whitespace for a thought-capture app, but also means dictation products have no precedent for a note/task pricing model.
6. **Growth channels (what is evidenced).** Wispr: word of mouth/PLG, endorsements, referral (month for month), founder content, UGC, paid social, India localisation. Superwhisper: founder network and developer endorsements, enterprise partnership (Cohere). Typeless/Wispr: Product Hunt. VoiceInk: open-source GitHub stars. Only Wispr has meaningful published evidence; for others channels are NOT FOUND.
7. **Funding bifurcation.** Wispr ($361M, $2B) and everyone else tiny or bootstrapped; indie bootstrapped companies (Superwhisper, MacWhisper, VoiceInk) survive on one-time/lifetime sales and modest subscription. Menlo's thesis in the TNW report: free dictation existed for a decade with little adoption; value comes from acting on intent (that is exactly the structured-notes/tasks layer).
8. **Threat: big-tech free offline.** Google's Eloquent (free, no caps, offline Gemma) launched on iOS April 2026 with an Android reference in its listing; if it ships on Android, plain dictation is free and offline from Google.
