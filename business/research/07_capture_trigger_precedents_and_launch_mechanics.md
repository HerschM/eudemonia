## 3. Android Quick-Capture Entry Points Without Accessibility Permission

Key platform rule (applies to every entry point below that starts a microphone foreground service): apps cannot create a foreground service of type microphone from the background, except in listed situations. Official exemption list for while-in-use permissions (microphone, camera, location): "A system component starts the service." "The service starts by interacting with app widgets." "The service starts by interacting with a notification." "The service starts as a PendingIntent that is sent from a different, visible app." "The service starts by an app which provides the VoiceInteractionService." Quick Settings tiles, headset buttons and the notes role are NOT in that list. A separate list of background-start exemptions includes "The user performs an action on a UI element related to your app. For example, they might interact with a bubble, notification, widget, or activity." [OFFICIAL] https://developer.android.com/develop/background-work/services/fgs/restrictions-bg-start (no date shown).
